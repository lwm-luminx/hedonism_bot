# Google Play listing (en-US)

**App name (30):** Chip by Lumière

**Short description (80):** Upload camera-card RAW and phone photos straight to your Lumière archive.

**Full description:**

Chip sends a photo shoot from your camera card or phone to your Lumière archive, without going through a computer.

• Plug in an SD card reader and pick the card or its DCIM folder. Chip finds every RAW (Sony ARW, DNG), HEIF and JPEG photo and keeps RAW + JPEG pairs together.
• Take photos with your phone's camera, or add them from your photo library. Original DNG, HEIC, JPEG and PNG files are kept as they are.
• Name the album, event and venue, then upload. Chip checks every file against your archive first and only sends what's new, so you can upload the same card again without duplicates.
• Record your shoot location while you work, so photos can be matched to venues later. Recording shows a notification and stops when you tell it to.
• Sign in with Facebook or a one-time device code, and switch between the archives you manage.

Chip is for photographers and teams who run a Lumière archive. You'll need an account on a Lumière server to upload.

## Data safety notes

- Collected: photos (user-selected, uploaded to the person's own archive), approximate and precise location (only while recording a shoot, sent with uploads), account info from Facebook sign-in (on the server).
- Encrypted in transit (HTTPS only). Data is not sold or shared with third parties. Users can request deletion at the URL above.
- Location foreground service declaration: records the shoot's location while the person is photographing, started by the person, with an ongoing notification and a Stop action.
