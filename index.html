A little more clarity

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Is Lumiere one photographer’s portfolio?

No. Lumiere is a platform for multiple independent photographers. Each photographer has their own gallery and workspace, served from a dedicated Lumiere subdomain or registered custom domain.

How do I find my photographer’s gallery?

Use the address your photographer gave you, or enter their Lumiere name above. There isn’t a public photographer directory here; your photographer’s shared link is the best place to start.

Can photographers use their own domain?

Yes. Lumiere supports registered custom domains as well as photographer addresses under lumiere.host.

Where do photographers sign in?

Open your own photographer’s gallery address, then use its sign-in option. Accounts and administration belong to the photographer’s workspace.
