03 / Beyond the upload

The details matter.
Keep them close.

Give each shoot its context, and give a growing archive room to breathe.

SHOOT CONTEXT

More than a folder name.

Enter the album, event, and venue in Chip so the details travel with your upload. Pick the photographer account for the shoot before sending files.

OPTIONAL LOCATION

Remember where it happened.

Choose to record your shoot location in Chip. With matching camera timestamps and location coverage, Lumière can suggest a configured venue for a photograph. Start and stop recording yourself, and delete saved history from your phone.

ARCHIVE STORAGE

Space for what comes next.

Move originals to configured archive storage while JPEG previews stay in the gallery. Connect folders through Cogsworth to see which Mac and storage locations are online in your workspace.

A little more clarity

Good questions.
Simple answers.

How do I get Chip or Cogsworth?

Chip is the iPhone and iPad companion; Cogsworth runs on Mac. Both connect to an existing Lumière photographer workspace. Public download links will be added here when available. If you already have an app installed, sign in with your photographer account or use a device code provided by your workspace administrator.

Does Chip keep uploading with the screen locked?

Keep Chip open and the card connected during uploads. If an upload is interrupted, open Chip and retry; completed files are remembered. Optional shoot location recording can continue in the background, but photo uploads need the app in the foreground.

How does shoot location work?

Recording is opt-in. Chip saves location history on your phone and sends it with uploads to help match photographs to configured venues. Your camera needs an accurate clock and a timezone offset in its capture metadata. Missing or ambiguous matches stay unresolved. Deleting phone history does not remove history already sent with uploads.

Does connecting a NAS move my originals?

Connecting a local or mounted NAS folder in Cogsworth registers its availability in your workspace. It does not transfer files. Album archive and restore use your separately configured server archive storage; gallery previews remain available.

Is Lumière one photographer’s portfolio?

No. Lumière is a platform for multiple independent photographers. Each photographer has their own gallery and workspace, served from a dedicated Lumière subdomain or registered custom domain.

How do I find my photographer’s gallery?

Use the address your photographer gave you, or enter their Lumière name above. There isn’t a public photographer directory here; your photographer’s shared link is the best place to start.

Can photographers use their own domain?

Yes. Lumière supports registered custom domains as well as photographer addresses under lumiere.host.

Where do photographers sign in?

Open your own photographer’s gallery address, then use its sign-in option. Accounts and administration belong to the photographer’s workspace.
